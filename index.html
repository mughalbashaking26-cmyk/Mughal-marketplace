SHOP SMART · STAY SAFE

Safety, privacy & marketplace rules

Clear rules help buyers and sellers use Mughal Marketplace responsibly.

Safe buying and selling

  • Meet in a busy public place and, where possible, bring someone you trust.
  • Inspect the item and test it before paying.
  • Never share an OTP, PIN, password or banking login.
  • Do not pay an advance to an unknown seller just to “reserve” an item.
  • Keep messages and payment receipts in case you need to report a problem.
  • Report suspicious listings, impersonation, counterfeit goods or misleading descriptions.

Privacy policy — preview draft

This prototype may store listings, saved items, profile details, notifications and reports in your browser on this device. This preview does not send that information to a central server. Avoid entering sensitive personal information.

Before publication, the owner must define what data is collected, why it is needed, who can access it, how long it is retained, how users can request deletion, and which hosting or messaging providers process it.

Terms of use — preview draft

  • Users must provide honest descriptions, accurate prices and permission to sell listed items.
  • Illegal, stolen, counterfeit, dangerous or otherwise prohibited goods are not allowed.
  • Buyers and sellers are responsible for checking items and agreeing on safe handover arrangements.
  • Mughal Marketplace is a listing platform concept; this preview does not process payments or guarantee transactions.
  • Listings or accounts may be restricted after a valid safety report when the production moderation system is in place.

How to report a problem

Open an item and choose Report. In this preview, reports are saved locally for demonstration only. On the published website, reports should be delivered to the admin moderation queue and reviewed by an authorized moderator.

These policy sections are starter drafts, not legal advice. Review and adapt them for your real service and applicable Pakistani laws before publication.

SHOP SMART, STAY SAFE

A few important safety tips

🛡️ For buyers

  • Inspect the item in person before paying where possible.
  • Meet in a public, well-lit place and take a trusted person with you.
  • Never share passwords, OTPs or banking PINs.
  • Be cautious of advance-payment pressure and deals that seem too good to be true.

🤝 For sellers

  • Use your own photos and describe flaws honestly.
  • Do not post your home address or private documents.
  • Confirm payment in your own account before handing over an item.
  • Do not list stolen, prohibited or counterfeit goods.
